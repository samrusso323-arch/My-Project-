Settings & history
  • Keep the phone completely still. If it moves, tap Calibrate again.
  • Even, steady light helps a lot. Avoid flickering lights and moving shadows.
  • Stand clear of the board between throws so the camera sees each dart land.
  • After three darts, pull them out. Oche spots the board is clear and moves to the next player. If someone misses the board, tap Add miss.
